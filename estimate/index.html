Already have plans?

The estimators are for budgeting. For a hard number we want to see the drawing set.

Plan-set quote

Email plans, specs and any addenda to info@greencommllc.com or upload from the contact page. A priced proposal comes back the same day, with a same-day COI.

No drawings?

For renovations and existing buildings, send photos and a rough count, or ask for a site walk. Where the drawings don't show wireless, cameras or door access, we lay them out and price each as an alternate.

Ranges aren't quotes

The estimator numbers are budget ranges at our current pricing. They don't include travel outside the St. Louis and Kansas City metros or unusual access. Fixed-price commercial projects start at $5,000; smaller jobs run on time and materials.

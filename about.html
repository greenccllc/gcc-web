How we work

Bid as drawn, back the same day

Send the drawings and a priced proposal comes back the same day. We don't pepper you with questions during the bid window: scope, counts, exclusions and any allowance are stated on the page.

Cat6A plenum on every drop

Baseline, with no upcharge. Tested with a Fluke DSX-8000 and labeled at both ends.

Nights and weekends, no premium

Standard rate at any hour. In an occupied school, clinic or store, we schedule around your calendar at no extra charge.

Two-person minimum on site

Never a solo technician. A foreman is on every crew.

We start where the electrician stops

You keep conduit, boxes and power. We carry everything from the pull string on: cable, terminations, racks, devices, testing and labels.

Plain-spoken proposals

What's in. What's out. Real numbers, whole dollars, firm for 30 days. Base scope and alternates priced separately so you can pick.

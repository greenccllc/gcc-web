What it does for the crew

Phone-first. Big buttons. Nobody reads a manual on a ladder.

Clock in where you're standing

The job you're at is the top button. Tap it. Breaks are one more tap. Forgot to clock out? The office fixes it and the reason is saved — no arguments on Friday.

Pay day in plain English

"Next check: Friday — about $1,240 take-home." Hours by day, hours by job, and a PAID stamp the moment the money is sent. Venmo username on the profile, so nobody texts to ask.

The gate code is on the job

Sticky notes on every job: crew notes for the crew, office notes for the office, private notes for you. Photos, plans and the PO right next to them. Your foreman will never say "I texted you the gate code" again.

Schedule that looks like the calendar

Month view with who's where and how many hours — the same layout as the calendar on our invoices, because it is that calendar. Plan Friday in ten seconds.

To-dos that live on the job

"Pick up the splicing case at Adams." "Pole 137 needs a vault." Ticked off from the truck, seen by the office.

Only your own numbers

Crew see their hours, their pay, their jobs. Foremen too. Owners and office admins see everything. Nothing leaks sideways.

What it does for the office

The boring parts happen while you sleep.

Calls and texts file themselves

Your work line's calls and texts land on the right job as notes — the AI keeps only what matters: decisions, dates, prices, problems. Call recordings get transcribed too. Personal line never touched.

Email finds its job

The Baldwin thread lands on Baldwin. Attachments come along; invoices and POs stay office-only automatically.

QuickBooks, done nightly

Contractor bills, the GC's invoice, time on every job — written to QuickBooks for you. Close the pay period and the punches lock.

Buddy Punch and GC portals

Your GC wants their clock? One click exports what they need in their shape, with what still needs adding on their side.

Period profit, not just cost

Billable minus crew cost, live. The owner's hours count as profit, because they are.

Payroll workbook + WH-347

Excel out, certified-payroll shape included. Your accountant will send a thank-you note.

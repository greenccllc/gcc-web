What you get back

Counts you can trust

Drops, cameras, doors, APs, racks — per sheet and per floor, with the symbol legend it learned from. Recounts when a sheet changes; firm numbers stay firm.

Footage and materials

Cable footage from the actual pathways, panels and patch cords sized, plenum where the spec (or the duct) demands it.

Scope in your words

Inclusions, exclusions, allowances vs. firm quantities, phasing. The clarifications you'd write anyway — drafted for you.

Pricing that's been calibrated

Labor and material benchmarks tuned on real jobs, adjustable to your book. The proposal comes out priced, not "TBD".

Bonfire and portal bids

New: Bonfire bid intake. Invites, addenda and due dates pulled into the board so nothing is missed because it was in a portal you forgot the password to.

Review cards, not a wall of text

One card per question the estimate raises. Approve, fix, or re-run a sheet. Then send — the design-build packet goes out as PDF.

What we install

UniFi G6 cameras

Turret, bullet and dome, interior and exterior. Cabled, mounted, aimed and focused. PoE only — no DC injectors hidden behind drywall.

UniFi Access doors

Door hub, reader, electric strike, exit button and door contact, with credentials and schedules programmed. Card, fob or phone. Our design basis: a reader on every exterior door plus IT, storage and other secure rooms.

IP intercom + paging

IP intercom and entry stations. Overhead paging speakers, zoned by floor or department.

Recorders + retention

Recorder and drives sized to your camera count for 30-day retention, with remote viewing set up. A camera package from us is complete: PoE switching, patching, recorder and commissioning.

Detection

Person, vehicle and license-plate detection runs locally on the recorder, keeping footage off the cloud. Alerts by email or push.

Your cameras or ours

Cameras and door hardware are often furnished by the owner. Either way works: we price the cabling, mounting, aiming and configuration, and furnish the devices when you want one number. Camera and access runs are yellow so the next technician can tell them from data.

What we don't bid

Listing the exclusions up front saves a re-scope after award.

Fire alarm

Out of scope. We coordinate with the FA contractor, but we don't bid head-end equipment, devices, or AHJ closeout.

Area-of-rescue (AoR)

Out of scope. UL 2017 / IBC 1009 systems are coordinated like fire alarm — the AoR contractor owns head-end and station install, AHJ closeout, and integration testing.

Mass notification beyond standard PA

If the spec calls for ECS / EVAC integrated with FA, that lives with the FA scope.

Nurse call, radio boosters, lighting and HVAC controls

Not ours. Nurse call, public-safety radio (BDA / DAS), lighting controls and building automation each belong to their own specialty trade.

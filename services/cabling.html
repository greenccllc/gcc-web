Scope at a glance

Cat6A plenum, every drop

Standard on every project, at no upcharge. Pulled, terminated at both ends, labeled and traced. Multi-port outlets, floor boxes and poke-thrus included.

Fiber backbone

Multimode for in-building backbones, single-mode between buildings and across a campus. Where a job has an MDF and an IDF, the backbone between them is fiber. Fusion-terminated at $50 per strand end, all-in: connector, splice, tray dressing, OTDR trace and record.

Telecom rooms (TIA-607 / 569)

MDF and IDF racks and cabinets, patch panels, cable managers, ladder rack, bonding and grounding, UPS and PDUs. Built out, dressed and labeled.

Certification + as-builts

Fluke DSX-8000 results on every drop, OTDR on every strand. TIA-606 labels at both ends. Marked-up as-builts and the test reports at closeout — included, not a line item.

Wi-Fi design + survey

Coverage layouts designed in-house. Access point cabling, mounting and configuration. If the drawings show data outlets but no wireless, we lay it out and price it as an alternate.

What stays with the electrician

Conduit, boxes and power. We pick up at the stubbed pathway and the pull string, and carry everything from there: cable, terminations, devices and racks.

Standards we hold to

BICSI / TIA-568

Cabling performance, channel and permanent-link models, application support.

TIA-606-B

Labeling and identifier scheme. Both ends of every cable, both ends of every patch cord, plus the administration record.

TIA-607

Grounding and bonding for telecom rooms. Bonded TGB, ladder bonded to TGB, equipment bonded back.

NEC / UL

Plenum jackets in air-handling spaces. UL-listed components only. Listed firestop where the cable crosses a rated assembly.

Printed labels

Heat-shrink for fiber, vinyl wrap for copper. Printed on-site, never hand-written.

Fluke DSX-8000

Channel + permanent link. LinkWare reports per drop. Lifetime workmanship warranty on our cabling.
